PocketLab

Don't just learn it. Test it.

Turn what you don't understand into something you can see, explore, and understand. Visuals explain; a deterministic simulator, not an image model, decides the physics.

Predict

Pick which sphere lands first and why. Your answer is recorded before anything runs.

Experiment

Switch vacuum and air, change mass, height and gravity. The animation is the physics state.

Understand

Compare your prediction with the result, see the forces, then apply the idea to a new case.

    Results are simulated with quadratic drag (air density 1.2 kg/m³, sphere Cd 0.47, diameter 0.10 m), not measured in a real lab. Scene backgrounds are drawn in code; this build has no AI image provider, so no generated images are shown and none are faked. A server-side image/vision provider can be added later behind an API route.
