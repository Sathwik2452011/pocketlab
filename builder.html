PocketLab demo builder

Make your demo images in ChatGPT (or anywhere), then add them here with labels, a quiz and experiments. Download saved.json and upload it to the root of your GitHub repo. Nothing here is sent anywhere; it all stays in your browser.

Tips for the ChatGPT images: ask for a cinematic 16:9 illustration, and add "no text, letters, numbers or labels in the image" because PocketLab draws the labels on top. For each experiment, ask for "the same scene and composition, but [the change]" so before and after look alike. Keep one variable with 2 or 3 options per question to limit how many images you need.
